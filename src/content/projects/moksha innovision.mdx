## The brief

Moksha Innovision is the annual fest of NSUT Delhi. In 2023 it drew around **80,000 attendees**. The site had to handle event discovery and registration for all of them, and give event organisers a way to run their own events without asking the web team for help.

## What it does

**For organisers**

- Create events, each with its own registration form.
- Track who has registered from a dashboard.

**For participants**

- Register for events, alone or as a team.
- See every event you're part of and who your teammates are.

Access is **role-based**, so organisers only see and manage their own events.

## The look

The theme blends traditional and modern, with mandala effects throughout and 3D touches built in Three.js.

## Under the hood

- **Next.js** and **TypeScript**.
- **Three.js** for the 3D effects.
- **Supabase** for auth, the database and role-based access.
