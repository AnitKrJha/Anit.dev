**The ChessMaster** is a real-time multiplayer chess game you play in the browser with friends. You create a room, send the invite, and the game happens live: every move shows up instantly for both players and for anyone watching. You can [play it here](https://thechessmaster.vercel.app/), and the [code is on GitHub](https://github.com/anitkrjha/chessmaster).

## Why I built it

I've always loved chess, and building a way to play it with my friends felt like the perfect project.

It's also a genuinely real-time problem. Most web apps are fine if data shows up a second late. A chess game isn't. If your opponent moves and you don't see it, the game is broken.

## How it works

1. **Create an account.** You land on your own dashboard.
2. **See your history.** The dashboard lists the games you've played.
3. **Open a game room** and invite your friends.
4. **The first friend to join is your opponent.**
5. **Everyone else who joins is a spectator.** They watch the game live, move by move. It's like having your own live audience.

## The real-time part

Two pieces do most of the work:

- **[chess.js](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js)** knows the rules. It checks whether a move is legal and tracks the full game state: whose turn it is, check, checkmate, stalemate and draws. Chess has plenty of edge cases (castling, en passant, promotion), and chess.js handles all of them.
- **[Supabase Realtime](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime)** keeps everyone in the room in sync over [WebSockets](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebSocket). A move goes out to the opponent and every spectator straight away, with no refreshing and no polling.

Supabase is also the database behind the app.

## Under the hood

- **Next.js** and **[TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)**
- **Supabase** for the database and Realtime
- **chess.js** for move validation and game state
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)** for the interface
- Deployed on **Vercel**
