## Why I built it

I play a lot of chess, and I wanted a way to play friends without either of us signing up for a big platform. I also wanted an excuse to build something properly real-time.

## How it works

- **Create an account** and land on a dashboard with your game history.
- **Open a game room** and share the invite with friends.
- **The first friend to join is your opponent.** Everyone else who joins becomes a spectator and watches the game move by move.

## Under the hood

- **[TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)** and **Next.js** for the app.
- **[Supabase Realtime](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/realtime)** over [WebSockets](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebSocket) to sync moves between players and spectators.
- **[chess.js](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js)** for move validation and game state.
- **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)** for the interface.
