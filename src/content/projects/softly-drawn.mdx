## The brief

**Softly Drawn** is the portfolio studio of **Kio**, a digital artist who makes semi-realistic character art, original characters, book covers and profile icons. Every piece is hand-drawn, with no AI. The site had to feel like a small gallery: quiet enough that the artwork leads, but with enough craft that it's memorable.

## What I built

- **A WebGL hero.** A three.js scene with custom shaders, loaded only on desktop and code-split so phones get a fast static version.
- **Page transitions that carry the artwork.** Click a piece and its image morphs from the grid into its own page, using GSAP Flip.
- **Scroll-driven reveals** with GSAP ScrollTrigger and SplitText, plus Lenis for smooth scrolling.
- **A contact page that reads like a letter** on cream paper, for commissions and orders.
- **Reviews copied word for word** from Fiverr, each linked to the artwork it was about.

## Under the hood

- **Vite**, **React 19** and **TypeScript**, with react-router for `/`, `/work`, `/work/:slug` and `/contact`.
- An **image pipeline** script that takes the full-resolution PNGs and writes responsive WebP sizes, GPU textures and blur placeholders, then updates a manifest the site reads.
- All content (bio, links, artworks, reviews) lives in typed data files, so adding a new piece never means touching a component.
- Fraunces and Manrope variable fonts, self-hosted.
