# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- Recruiters, hiring managers and engineers who reach anit.dev from LinkedIn, GitHub or a résumé link.
- Developers who land on a blog post from search (Git, PowerShell, GRUB, Java NPTEL).
- Clients and friends checking out work Anit has built for them.

Scene: an engineer opens anit.dev from a LinkedIn message on a laptop in the evening, or on a phone between meetings. In about twenty seconds they want to know who Anit is, where Anit works, what Anit is good at, and whether the work looks careful.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of **Anit Jha**, a 23-year-old DevOps, Tools & Automation Engineer at Apple Online Store (AOS). It introduces Anit, shows selected work, hosts the blog, and links to socials. Success means a visitor leaves knowing Anit's role and stack, opens a project or a post, and can find the site easily in search.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, precise, luminous.** An infrastructure engineer who also cares how things look. Friendly first person, short sentences, a little playful ("DevOps plus automation, or whatever we're calling it this year"), never boastful.

## Anti-references

- Terminal-cosplay dev portfolios: green-on-black, fake shell prompts, monospace everything.
- SaaS landing clichés: hero-metric stat rows, identical icon-card grids, glassmorphism, gradient text.
- Rainbow-shadow buttons and loud neon glows.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: italic display serifs, rule-separated columns, tiny tracked labels everywhere.

## Design Principles

1. **Simple, then pristine.** One column, few elements, each one finished with care.
2. **The gradient is the signature.** A soft aurora lives at the top of every page. Everything below it is calm.
3. **Content over chrome.** Real work, real writing, real photos. No filler sections.
4. **Findable.** Every page has a unique title, description, canonical URL and structured data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast in both themes, visible focus rings, semantic landmarks.
- Theme follows the OS until the visitor picks one.
- All motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
